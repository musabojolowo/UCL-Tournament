📋 Tournament Rules

1. Tournament Format

  • 16 teams divided into 4 groups (A–D) of 4 teams each.
  • Each team plays every other team in their group twice (home & away) — 6 matches per team.
  • 12 matches per group · 48 total group matches.
  • Top 2 teams per group advance to the Knockout Stage (8 teams).

2. Points System

  • Win = 3 pts · Draw = 1 pt · Loss = 0 pts
  • Tiebreaker: Points → Goal Difference → Goals Scored

3. Knockout Stage

  • Quarter-Final seeding: 1st overall vs 8th, 2nd vs 7th, 3rd vs 6th, 4th vs 5th (ranked by Pts → GD → GF across all 4 groups).
  • All knockout rounds (QF, SF) are played over TWO LEGS — aggregate score decides the winner.
  • If aggregate is level after Leg 2 → Extra Time → Penalty Shoot-out.
  • The Grand Final is a single leg. If level after 90 mins → Extra Time → Penalties.

4. Match Settings

  • Duration: 10 Minutes
  • Extra Time: ON · Penalties: ON
  • Substitutions: 6 · Injuries: OFF

5. 📶 Disconnection Rules

  • 0–5 Minutes: Replay the full 10-minute match.
  • 5–10 Minutes: Replay an 8-minute match.
  • 10–20 Minutes: Replay a 7-minute match.
  • 20–30 Minutes: Replay a 6-minute match.
  • 30–40 Minutes: Replay a 5-minute match.
  • 40–50 Minutes: Replay the 1st Half (5 minutes) only.
  • 50–70 Minutes: Replay the 1st Half (5 minutes) only.
  • 70–90 Minutes:
    • If the winning player disconnects → replay the 1st Half (5 minutes).
    • If the losing player disconnects → match result stands, match ends.
  • If a player intentionally quits while losing, the winning player will be awarded 3 additional goals.
  • Repeated intentional disconnections or attempts to manipulate results may result in a 3–0 forfeit, points deduction, or disqualification — at the discretion of the tournament organizers.
  • The tournament organizers' decision on all disconnection cases is final.

6. Match Results

  • A screenshot of the full-time match result must be posted in the official tournament WhatsApp group immediately after the match.
  • Tag an admin when posting the result.
  • Only verified results will be recorded in the table.

7. Player Conduct

  • Respect all players and tournament administrators at all times.
  • Insults, harassment, cheating, exploitation of game bugs, or any form of unsporting behavior will not be tolerated.
  • Any player found guilty of misconduct may face: match forfeiture, point deduction, suspension, or disqualification.
  • The decisions of the tournament organizers are final and binding.
